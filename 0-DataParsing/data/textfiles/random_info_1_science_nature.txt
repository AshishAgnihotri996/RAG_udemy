RANDOM INFORMATION FILE 1: SCIENCE AND NATURE
==============================================

1. Honey almost never spoils. Edible honey has been found in ancient Egyptian tombs.

2. Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood.

3. A day on Venus (one full rotation) is longer than a year on Venus.

4. Botanically, bananas are berries, but strawberries are not.

5. Sharks have been around longer than trees. Sharks date back more than 400 million years.

6. Lightning strikes somewhere on Earth roughly 100 times every second.

7. Adult humans have 206 bones, while babies are born with around 270 or more that fuse over time.

8. Wombats produce cube-shaped droppings.

9. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

10. Sea otters sometimes hold hands while sleeping so they do not drift apart.

11. Antarctica is the largest desert on Earth, because deserts are defined by low precipitation, not heat.

12. A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance."

13. Dolphins sleep with one half of their brain at a time.

14. Tardigrades (water bears) can survive extreme heat, cold, radiation, and even the vacuum of space.

15. The Moon is slowly drifting away from Earth at about 3.8 centimeters per year.

16. Water is unusual because it expands when it freezes, which is why ice floats.

17. The human body contains an estimated 37 trillion cells.

18. Scientists theorize that it may rain diamonds deep inside planets like Neptune and Uranus.

19. Crows can recognize individual human faces and remember them for years.

20. Bamboo is a type of grass, and some species can grow almost a meter in a single day.

21. The heart of a blue whale is roughly the size of a small car.

22. Butterflies taste with their feet.

23. A single bolt of lightning is hotter than the surface of the Sun.

24. Trees in a forest can share nutrients through underground fungal networks.

25. Rainbows are actually full circles. From the ground we usually see only the top arc.
